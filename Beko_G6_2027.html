Timetable Analysis

  • Total weekly periods: 40 periods across 5 school days, 8 periods per day (4 before the break, 4 after), plus a morning Line Up from 7:40 to 8:05 and a Break from 11:05 to 11:35.
  • Arabic is the most frequent subject, with double periods on: Sunday, Monday (twice), Wednesday, and Thursday.
  • Science has a double period on Tuesday.
  • English has a double period only on Sunday.
  • Tuesday afternoon is the only session split into 4 fully separate periods with no merging.
  • Art, Skills, P.E., and Qur'an each appear only once per week.
